# KeyClip Landing

The public website that shows what the KeyClip Mac app does and hands out the download.

## Language

**Feature**:
Something KeyClip does, shown as one selectable entry in the list on the left.
_Avoid_: Tab, list item, bullet

**Stage**:
The large container on the right where the selected Feature is shown.
_Avoid_: Main container, right panel, hero

**Demo**:
The scripted motion piece in the Stage showing a user operating the KeyClip app for one Feature.
_Avoid_: Video, screenshot, mockup

**Device Mockup**:
The laptop or phone frame that KeyClip draws around a recording. It is an output of the app, never a depiction of the app itself.
_Avoid_: Mockup, frame

**Callout**:
A floating label attached to a point on a Demo.
_Avoid_: Tooltip, chip, pin

**Download Bar**:
The strip at the bottom of the Stage carrying the free message and the Download action.
_Avoid_: Footer, CTA bar

**Stories**:
The phone presentation that shows one Feature at a time, full screen, moved through by tapping either side.
_Avoid_: Carousel, slides

**Feature List**:
The presentation that keeps every Feature title visible at once. Used on desktop, and held in reserve for phones.
_Avoid_: Tabs, menu

**Tap Hint**:
The one-time message telling a phone visitor which side of the screen to tap.
_Avoid_: Tutorial, onboarding
